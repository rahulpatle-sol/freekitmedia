# FreeKit Media - User Guide

## 1. Who this guide is for
- Visitors exploring FreeKit Media services.
- Businesses considering a partnership / audit.
- Anyone who wants to understand how the agency site works and how to move from discovery to enquiry.

## 2. How to use the website

### Step 1: Discover
- Open the homepage and scroll through hero, partners marquee, services, proof, process.
- Use the navbar to jump to sections: Services, Process, Partners, FAQ, Locations.

### Step 2: Explore services
- Go to /services to see what is offered: Content, Ads, Website/Social, Personal branding.
- Open a specific service like /services/content-creation-branding for details.

### Step 3: Explore proof / case studies
- Go to /case-studies for written outcomes.
- Go to /partners for partner brands and their work.
- Open /partners/[slug] for the full story: brand hero, story, deliverables, gallery, strategy, results.

### Step 4: Pick your path
- Ready to start? Use the final CTA: "Request a free audit" -> /contact.
- Or chat on WhatsApp via floating / footer buttons.

### Step 5: Enquiry
- Fill the contact form or use WhatsApp / phone / email.
- The team responds with next steps; audit is a relaxed 30-minute call.

## 3. For partners / business owners
- Find your brand page under /partners.
- Read the story, deliverables, gallery and results.
- Use the Instagram links inside the gallery to watch the reel.

## 4. Notes
- All case-study data is content-driven from `content/partners.ts`.
- Optional sections render only when data exists; missing metrics fall back to deliverables.
